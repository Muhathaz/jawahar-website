The systems, one after another.

01 / Surveillance

Cameras, recording, and a room that watches.

Analog and IP cameras, automatic number-plate recognition, thermal views for dark yards, and recording on DVR, NVR or NAS. Control rooms are laid out for the people who will sit there. Perimeter lines and guard patrol follow the route a guard can actually walk. A live construction site can carry a time-lapse camera until handover.

02 / Access and entrance

The door, the gate, and the phone that opens them.

Cards, codes and biometrics. Video door phones with a picture, an electric lock, and release from a phone. Boom barriers, sliding and swing gates, automatic sliding glass doors and automatic swing doors, specified so the lobby and the car park tell the same story.

03 / Retail protection

A line at the door, and a count of the floor.

Radio-frequency and acousto-magnetic pedestals, with people counting where the shop wants to know the day as well as protect the stock. This is separate from guard patrol: patrol is a route and a record, not a copy of the shop system.

Security systems for buildings in Qatar.
