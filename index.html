07 / Questions

A few useful starting points.

Which system should we talk about first? +

Start with the decision in front of you: a new building, a tender, or a failed camera line. We will say which parts of the scope belong together.

Do you only install, or do you maintain as well? +

Both. Survey, design, supply, installation and maintenance are one sequence. A site can also call us for an existing system.

Can you look after printers and office supplies? +

Yes, as a quieter line of work: printer and copier repair, computers, a small network, and paper, toner and desk supplies by quote. There is no cart.
